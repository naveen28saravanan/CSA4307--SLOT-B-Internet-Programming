Any engineering student with a valid college ID.

No. Registration is free and includes lunch and a participation certificate.

What happens after I submit the form?

The browser sends your details to the server in a POST request. The server replies with a confirmation, and we email you a registration ID.

Yes. Write to the helpdesk before 10 November.
